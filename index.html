How the house works

1

Players play

Every game pays by its real odds minus a small house edge. In dice, a 50% roll pays 1.96× instead of 2×.

2

The pool covers every bet

One shared bankroll takes the other side of every bet, in every game. When players lose, the pool grows. When they win, the pool pays them.

3

Holders share the result

Put funds in the pool and you own a slice of the house: its edge over time, and its bad nights too.

Built to be checked

Randomness you can verify

Every game result will come from Chainlink VRF, which publishes a proof with each number. Nobody, including us, can pick the outcome.

No bet can break the bank

A bet is refused if its maximum payout would take more than a set slice of the pool.

Paid only from profit

Fees come from net revenue in winning periods. Never from stakes, never in losing periods.

The pool, in public
